Delete your Seen account

Seen is developed by Seen Live App.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Seen and go to your Profile.
  2. Tap the ☰ menu (top right), then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Can't use the app?

Email seenlive.support@gmail.com from the email address on your Seen account with the subject "Delete my Seen account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, profile, and profile photo
  • Every game you've logged, and the photos you added to them
  • Your Must-See lists, scouting reports, stadium reviews, comments, likes, and follows

What stays

  • If you linked a game with a friend, their copy of that game stays on their account. Only yours is removed.
  • Game schedules and box scores aren't personal data and stay in Seen.

Nothing is kept after deletion except where the law requires it.

Delete some of your data (keep your account)

You can delete individual things at any time, right in the app. They're removed immediately and permanently:

  • A logged game: open the game's card, then Delete this game. This also removes that game's photos.
  • A photo: open the card's back and delete the photo.
  • A scouting report or stadium review: open it and choose Delete.
  • A comment: tap the trash icon on your comment.
  • A Must-See player, team, or stadium: Must-See list → Edit → tap ✕.
  • Someone you follow: open their profile and tap Unfollow.

To delete anything else (such as a community thread post), email seenlive.support@gmail.com from your account's email address and say what you'd like removed. We'll delete it within 30 days.

Want a copy first? Profile → ☰ menu → Settings → Export My Data.
